About Kavira

The idea

Kavira is a small, calm place for poets who write in English and Hindi. Every poet is placed in a circle of up to six poets who read and review each other's work.

Poetry grows when someone reads it closely. So reviews on Kavira are written with care, and they are public.

A poem is finished only when someone has read it.

How it works

  1. Create an account. You must be 16 or older.
  2. Write a poem of at least 80 words, and publish it.
  3. Read the poems of your circle.
  4. Leave a review of at least 30 words.

Made with

  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript only
  • Browser storage, so nothing leaves your computer
  • GitHub Pages for hosting

Read more about HTML on MDN.

The numbers

The limits Kavira keeps
What Limit Why
Poem length 80 words or more Long enough to be worth a review
Review length 30 words or more Thoughtful, not a one-line "nice"
Reviews per poem One per reader Keeps every voice fair
Circle size Up to 6 poets Small enough to know each other
Age 16 and over Community safety
