Community rules

Kavira works because poets read each other carefully. These rules keep it that way.

The basics

You must be 16 or older. We ask for your date of birth once, when you join.

Post only your own work. If someone else wrote it, do not post it.

Be kind and be specific. Criticise the poem, never the person.

Poems

At least 80 words. Spaces and line breaks do not count. Only words do.

One language per poem. Choose English or Hindi.

Reviews

At least 30 words. Say what worked, what moved you, and what you would try differently.

One review per poem. You cannot review your own poem.

Reviews are public. Everyone can read them.

Circles

You are placed in a circle of up to six poets who write in your language. Read the poems in your circle and review them.
